Last year I gave you three questions on one piece of paper and you had all three in fifteen minutes. I took notes. So this year I took the whole day, drove it two hours northeast, and hid it in a wooden house with a fire pit and a very quiet sky.

A year ago in Chiba we sat on two camping chairs under all those stars with a bottle between us, and I decided something about you that I have not changed my mind about since. I am aware of what I'm doing here. I'm doing it on purpose.
